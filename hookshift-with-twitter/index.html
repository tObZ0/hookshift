THE MECHANISM

Autonomy with boundaries.

01 / FUND

Treasury routing

Compatible token tax revenue can be routed to a treasury contract, subject to the launch platform's actual integration capabilities.

02 / DECIDE

Agent proposals

An AI agent reviews onchain activity and proposes a strategy change, recording the reasoning and intended outcome.

03 / EXECUTE

Onchain guardrails

Allowlisted actions, reserve floors, spending ceilings and approval delays restrict what any strategy can execute.

SAFETY BY DESIGN

The agent cannot rewrite its limits.

Our planned architecture separates the strategy proposer from the execution contract. The contract—not the AI—will validate permitted assets, recipients, spending caps, timing and emergency pauses. Real deployment requires contract tests and a security review.
